# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors are expected to communicate clearly, review changes in good faith, and keep technical discussions focused on the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct
- Assume technical intent, not bad intent
- Give actionable feedback
- Keep criticism tied to code, design, or process

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks
- Harassment
- Doxxing
- Threats
- Repeated disruptive behavior

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this standard.
